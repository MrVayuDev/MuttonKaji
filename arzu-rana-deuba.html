ARZU RANA DEUBA

The Bhutanese Refugee Audio & The Reward of Diplomacy

The NPR 25 Million Audio

In May 2023, while the fake Bhutanese refugee scam was exploding into public view, an audio recording surfaced. In it, Sandesh Sharma—who would later be arrested for his role in the scam—can be clearly heard outlining the flow of illegal, extortionate payments taken from Nepalis who were falsely promised refugee status in the United States.

In the recording, Sharma states unequivocally that 2.5 Crore to 3 Crore rupees (NPR 25-30 Million) was given directly to Arzu Rana Deuba, facilitated by Manju Khand (wife of Nepali Congress leader Bal Krishna Khand).

"...I have given two to three crore rupees to Arzu Rana and six crore to Manju Khand..."
— Extract from the Sandesh Sharma audio recording, May 2023

The scam was massive. High-ranking officials systematically forged documents to pass off wealthy Nepali citizens as genuine Bhutanese refugees, charging them exorbitant fees to traffic them to the USA. It was a scandal that exposed the rotting core of Nepal's political and bureaucratic nexus.

Investigation & Immunity

While Bal Krishna Khand, the Nepali Congress Home Minister during whose tenure the scam flourished, was arrested, and the CPN-UML's Top Bahadur Rayamajhi was also implicated and arrested, Arzu Rana Deuba enjoyed a different standard of justice.

Despite the widely circulated audio recording explicitly naming her as a recipient of millions in trafficking money, Arzu Rana Deuba was never formally charged. She maintained her innocence, quickly filing a cyber-crime complaint regarding the audio. The CIB later controversially claimed the audio was fabricated, though leaked details suggested the voice was genuine and simply taken out of a longer context. Public confidence in the police's clean chit was effectively zero.

The Diplomatic Reward

In Nepal's political ecosystem, proximity to the Prime Minister (her husband, Sher Bahadur Deuba) provides the ultimate shield. But the Nepali Congress didn't just shield Arzu Rana Deuba; they promoted her.

In July 2024, approximately 14 months after the refugee scam audio scandal broke, the Nepali Congress formed a new coalition government with the CPN-UML. In this new cabinet, Arzu Rana Deuba was appointed the Minister for Foreign Affairs.

The appointment was a masterclass in impunity. A politician publicly linked (via audio) to an international human trafficking and refugee forgery ring was chosen to represent Nepal on the global stage, meeting foreign dignitaries and managing international relations. It stood as a glaring symbol of Congress 0.2—a party that protects its elites at all costs.

The Gen-Z Protests & The Fire

The anger simmering over years of corruption and elite impunity boiled over during the September 2025 Gen-Z protests. Arzu Rana Deuba became a focal point of the crowds' rage.

While the Deuba-Oli government met the protesters with lethal force—resulting in 19 protesters shot dead by gunfire—the crowds targeted symbols of the ruling class. Ullens School in Lalitpur, seen by many as a symbol of elite, out-of-reach privilege and closely associated with Arzu Rana Deuba, was set on fire by protesters.

It was a destructive manifestation of deep societal rot: a political class that accumulates wealth through allegedly corrupt means while the youth they govern see no future but to bleed on the streets.
